Logo concepts

Three directions for one idea: one system, many brands. Every mark uses only the ink colour and the accent, so it re-tints with the site's skins and works in one colour. Shown on dark, light and accent, as a lockup with your name, as an app icon, and at real favicon sizes.

Concept A

Switch H

Your initial H, where the crossbar is a toggle switched on. It points straight at your signature: products that switch theme, language and brand from one system. Friendly and product-minded, and the knob stays readable as a dot even at 16px.

Concept B

Component H

A solid rounded tile with an H cut out of it, like a component chip from a design-system library. Bold and compact, it gives the strongest favicon and app icon of the three. It's also the most "systems" in feel, and the least playful.

Concept C

HT ligature

Both initials in one form: the right stem of the H rises into the T, so a single stroke belongs to both letters. Editorial and personal, it reads as a monogram rather than an app. It needs a bit more size to read, so it's weaker as a 16px favicon.
